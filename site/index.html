Does your recording give away where it was made?

Thunder and the drone of small planes turn up in the background of lots of videos. Public records log every lightning flash and almost every plane, with the exact time and place. Earwitness compares the sounds in a recording with those records to estimate where it was made.

It's a free, open-source tool for checking your own recordings. It runs on your computer and uploads nothing.

The audit lists the location clues it hears (thunder, propeller planes, mains hum, GPS and time tags) and what helps against each. The desktop app opens with the same check.

How it works

1Listen

Earwitness picks out thunderclaps, or the falling whine of a propeller plane as it passes overhead.

2Look up

It downloads the public records for that day: lightning flashes seen by a NOAA weather satellite, and plane tracks from adsb.lol.

3Compare

For each possible place and start time, it works out what a microphone there would have heard, and ranks the places that match the recording best.

Big storms and distant rumble defeated the thunder method, and a single common training plane was not enough. See every result, failures included.

How to protect a recording

  • Cut it. Cuts change the timing between sounds, and the timing is what gives the place away.
  • Remove the clock. On-screen clocks, timestamps and file metadata make the search much easier.
  • Strip the metadata. Phone videos often carry GPS coordinates, which give the answer away without any of this.
  • Don't count on filters or compression. The sounds survive both.

Responsible use

Earwitness exists to show that ordinary recordings leak location, to let people check their own, and to document what helps. It runs no online service, downloads no videos from links, and bundles no data. The project publishes no results that locate other people's recordings, and asks that you use it on your own recordings.

The method was demonstrated by colsto in three YouTube videos. His code is not public, so Earwitness is an independent reconstruction from those videos.
