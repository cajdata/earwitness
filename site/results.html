Results

Every test so far, the failures included. Each recording was searched without knowing the answer, and only then compared with where it was really made.

Real recordings

Twelve real recordings so far. Two contain propeller planes: colsto's own test recording, and a phone video made for this project. Ten contain thunder: public field recordings and camera clips shared with their locations. "Ranked 1st" means the true spot was the top answer. Results for other people's recordings are given without their places.

What the real recordings showed

  • Two planes plus a clock is a strong combination: it located a phone clip of 150 seconds.
  • One plane plus a clock works if the plane is unusual. A common training plane in a busy area is ambiguous at any clip length.
  • Without a clock, two planes were enough for colsto's recording but not for the phone video, made in a metro area full of training flights.
  • Thunder works only with close, distinct claps and little other lightning in the area searched. Big storms and distant rumble failed.

The synthetic tests are much easier than real life: no wind, traffic or speech, perfect clocks, and exact measurements. Real recordings are the test that counts.
